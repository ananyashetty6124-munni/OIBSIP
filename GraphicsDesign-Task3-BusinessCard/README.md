# Task 3 – Business Card Design

## Description

This folder contains a professional double-sided business card design created as part of the **OIBSIP Graphics Design Internship**.

The business card is designed to present professional contact and brand information in a clean, organized, and visually appealing format.

## Objective

The objective of this task is to create a professional double-sided business card that effectively communicates contact information while maintaining a consistent visual identity.

## Files

* `Ananya_Task3_Front.png` – Front side of the business card
* `Ananya_Task3_Back.png` – Back side of the business card
* `Ananya_Task3.pdf` – PDF version of the business card

## Design Specifications

* **Design Type:** Double-sided business card
* **Format:** PNG and PDF
* **Layout:** Front and back
* **Style:** Professional and modern
